Once upon a time, a curious engineer wanted to understand language models.
A language model reads tokens and predicts the next token. A transformer uses attention to compare earlier tokens.
During training, the model compares its predictions with the correct next character and updates its parameters.
The model contains embeddings, attention layers, feed-forward layers, normalization, and an output projection.
A small model with a tiny dataset has limited knowledge and generates imperfect text.
Building a tiny transformer helps the engineer understand tokenization, attention, training, weights, and sampling.
